# Project Defense Questions

Use these questions to check whether the project is understood. First try to answer from memory, then verify against the learning guide and code.

1. What is the difference between a bit and a symbol?
2. Why does 16-QAM carry four bits per symbol?
3. What does a constellation diagram show?
4. What are the in-phase and quadrature components?
5. Why is the average constellation energy normalized?
6. What is AWGN, and what real-world effects does this simplified model leave out?
7. What is Eb/N0, and why is it a useful comparison measure?
8. Why does the noise variance depend on the number of bits per symbol?
9. What does a hard-decision demodulator do?
10. How is BER calculated?
11. Why can a finite simulation show zero errors?
12. Why is Gray coding useful?
13. Why can BPSK and QPSK have similar BER at equal Eb/N0?
14. Why is 16-QAM generally more sensitive to noise than QPSK at equal Eb/N0?
15. Why might two languages produce different error counts even when their algorithms are correct?
16. What would need to change to model multipath fading?
17. Why is this a baseband simulation rather than an RF transmitter?
18. What part of the project did you personally test, modify, and validate?
